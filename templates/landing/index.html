How it works

Three steps, no app to download.

Your customers stay inside WhatsApp, and you stay in control of your calendar.

You publish your available times

Add single slots or a whole day at once from the dashboard. Customers can only book the times you list.

Customers message your number

They choose a service, give their name and pick a time from a numbered list. The bot confirms straight away.

You see every booking

Bookings appear in your dashboard with the customer's phone number. If a customer cancels, that time goes back on the list.

Features

What the bot does today.

Bookings from your own slots

No back-and-forth over dates. A time can only be booked once, so double-bookings cannot happen.

Instant confirmations

Each customer gets a confirmation with the service, date, time and location, taken directly from your records.

Self-service cancellations

Customers cancel their own bookings by message, and only ever their own. The freed time is offered to the next customer.

Stock enquiries

Customers can ask what is in stock and see names, prices and quantities from the list you maintain.

Owner dashboard

Manage bookings, adjust stock levels and publish time slots. Search bookings by name or phone number.

Simple metrics

See bookings per day, cancellations, popular services and times, and how many customers messaged the bot.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp number as they normally would, and the bot replies in the same chat.

Can customers book any time they like?

No. They can only book times you have published, so your calendar stays under your control.

Can customers cancel or change a booking?

They can cancel their own booking by messaging the bot. To change a time, they cancel and book a new one.

Do I need a special WhatsApp number?

The bot runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform, which needs a number that is not already registered with a regular WhatsApp account, plus a business verification step. We can guide you through it.

What does it cost?

It depends on your setup, and WhatsApp charges its own per-message fees. Get in touch and we will give you a clear quote.
