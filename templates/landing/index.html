FAQ

Common questions.

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp number as they normally would, and the assistant replies in the same chat.

What can it answer?

Stock, prices, where you are and when you’re open, based on the details set up for your business. Other topics are politely steered back to your business.

Is a real person replying?

No. Replies come from an automated assistant. It uses AI for open-ended questions, while stock lists, bookings and confirmations come straight from your records.

Do I have to use bookings?

No. Bookings are an extra for businesses that work by appointment. If that isn’t you, you can ignore them.

Do I need a special WhatsApp number?

Usually yes. The WhatsApp Business Platform typically needs a number that isn’t already registered with a regular WhatsApp account, plus a business verification step.

What does it cost?

It depends on your setup, and WhatsApp charges its own per-message fees. Get in touch and we will give you a quote.
